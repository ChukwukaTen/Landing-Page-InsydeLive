What you can do on InsydeLive

Check in
Check in when you arrive and see who else is there. The app confirms you are at the venue, so the list stays honest.
I'm going out
Tell the people who follow you where you're heading tonight, and see who else is out. You can also sell a few tickets for your night (up to 20), so friends and followers can buy a spot to come along.
Gatherings
Host your own invite-only house party, birthday or private event. Only the people you invite can see it. Guests reply in the app, and you can charge for entry or accept cash gifts.
Buy tickets
Buy event tickets in the app and show the QR code at the door. If you cannot go, send your ticket to a friend.
Send a gift
Buy someone at a venue a drink or a bottle. They get a code to redeem at the bar.
Order to your table
At venues and events that offer it, order food and drink from your phone and have it brought to you.
Stay safe
Save an emergency contact, let someone you trust see your status, and send an SOS to people at your venue.
Moments
Share photos and short videos from the night, tag your friends, and see what's happening at a venue right now.
Crews
Make a crew with your friends and see each other on a map while you're out, so nobody gets lost.
Messages
Chat with the people you meet and the friends you go out with.
Follow venues and organizers
Follow the places and promoters you love to see their posts, events and offers first.
Live events
Find concerts and other live events near you, and buy your tickets in the app.
Live Drops
Ask for a short live video from a venue to see what it's like inside before you go.
Tip your waiter
At venues that offer table service, leave a tip from your phone.

Run a venue, put on events, or host a gathering?

Anyone can host a gathering straight from the app.

Venues and event organizers apply from inside the app. Once you are approved you can:

  • list events and sell tickets, with QR check-in at the door
  • take food and drink orders and tips from your phone
  • post updates and send messages to your followers
  • give promoters their own codes and see what they sell
  • feature your venue or event, and see how your nights are going

Payouts go to your bank account in the UK, Europe, the US, Canada, Nigeria, Ghana, South Africa and Kenya. We're working to bring payouts to more countries, so if yours isn't listed yet, check back soon.
